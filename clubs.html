Clubs

Players: registrations by club, supplied by the county board (Galway Camogie for Galway camogie).

Club areas: built from Census 2022 electoral divisions. Each division is given to the nearest club, then checked by hand in the club area editor. Real club catchments follow parish lines and can overlap, so treat the areas as estimates.

Population: CSO Census 2022 Small Area Population Statistics by electoral division, by sex. The same 2022 figures are used for every season. Boundaries: CSO Electoral Divisions 2022 and county outlines © Tailte Éireann, CC BY 4.0; Northern Ireland © OpenStreetMap contributors.
